Bang for Your Buck: Protein Value by Store

This project compares Walmart, Target, and Whole Foods to find the most protein-efficient and cost-efficient foods for gym-goers. Each point shows a product's protein per dollar against how processed it is, so the best picks sit in the shaded region: more protein for the money, and less processing.
